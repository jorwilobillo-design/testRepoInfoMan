## 🚀 Project Overview
The **Hope, Inc. ERP** is a specialized web-based enterprise solution designed to streamline, centralize, and automate core business operations including Customer Management, Product Catalog, Human Resources, and Sales tracking. Built with a modern dark-themed UI and robust backend integration, it aims to deliver efficient workflow management and secure access for all organizational levels.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **Authentication & Role Management:** Secure login and registration modals with user session handling and role-based access.
* **Customer Management:** Centralized tracking and management of client contacts and accounts.
* **Product Catalog & Inventory:** Real-time inventory monitoring and product catalog management.
* **Sales & HR Operations:** Streamlined tracking of sales orders, revenues, and personnel data.
